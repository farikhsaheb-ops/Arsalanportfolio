BRAND SYSTEM / 2026

A consistent look.
Room to create.

This page documents the core visual language used by the Arsalan Warish portfolio: a precise monogram, expressive accent colors, clear typography and generous spacing.

01 / LOGO

Mark & wordmark

Use the full wordmark for wider placements and the monogram where space is limited. Keep breathing room around the mark; do not stretch or recolor it arbitrarily.

02 / COLOR

Night palette with a warm spark

Use the near-black as the structural base, violet for emphasis and citrus as a sparing action accent. Coral can add warmth to graphic work.

03 / TYPOGRAPHY

Distinctive, readable, functional

Space Grotesk — DisplayHeadlines · section titles · strong short statements
Manrope — InterfaceBody copy · navigation · buttons · forms
DM Mono — UtilityLabels · code · metadata · microcopy

04 / PRINCIPLES

How to keep it coherent

  • Prioritize clarity over decoration.
  • Use an 8px spacing rhythm for layouts.
  • Reserve gradients and glow for focal moments.
  • Keep body copy comfortable and concise.
  • Use concept labels honestly; publish client work only with permission.
